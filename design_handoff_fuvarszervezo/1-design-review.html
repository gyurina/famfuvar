Családi Fuvarszervező

A full read of the shipped UI — seven screens, three sheets, the shell — and a proposal for what the app should be organised around instead.

Method
Every “Most” frame below is rebuilt from the source files, not from screenshots. Values, spacing and copy are the real ones. Family data is the seed household (Gyuri, Judit, Papi, Mami, Nani, Simi, Janka).
Scope
Concept, information architecture and visual system. The small-screen layout bugs already listed in sprint-uiux-review.md are not repeated here — they are real, and orthogonal to this.
Language
Interface copy stays Hungarian. Commentary is in English.

The one thing

The app is built as a calendar that happens to track drivers. In practice a family opens it for one reason: something has no driver and someone has to decide who. Every screen currently reports that state well and does almost nothing to resolve it — the Ma screen announces “2 gazdátlan láb ma” and then renders the two orphan legs as flat, untappable cards. Assigning a driver is possible on exactly one screen, behind a small pill, an expanding panel and two steps.
A household has five drivers. Five faces fit across a phone. The proposal below turns assignment from a flow into a tap, and rebuilds the navigation around that.

Findings

01
The orphan legs on “Ma” are not tappableKRITIKUS
Ma.tsx renders “? Nincs sofőr” as a plain div with no handler. The screen that raises the alarm is the one screen that cannot act on it. The user has to remember the tab name, switch to Fuvartábla, find the same row again.
02
Two tabs show the same weekKRITIKUS
Fuvartábla and Hét both page through the same seven days with the same ◀ Ma ▶ control, over the same rows — one keyed on legs, one on occurrences. Nothing in either header explains the split, and the difference is a data-model distinction, not a user one.
03
Database words are in the interfaceKRITIKUS
„láb”, „gazdátlan”, „occurrence generálás”, „42 sor generálva”, „Körút”, „Hatókör”. No parent says any of these. Nine of them are visible without scrolling on the Sablon screen alone.
04
Assignment costs two steps and a reflowFONTOS
Tap pill → panel opens and pushes the list down → “Ki vezet?” → “Ki megy még?” → two optional checkboxes → confirm. Six decisions for the common case, which is “Judit, ahogy mindig”.
05
Emoji are doing the job of an icon setFONTOS
☀️🚗📅🎯📋 in the nav, ⚡🔗🤒🚶📍✏️⊘⋯◀▶ in content. They mix full-colour vendor art with mono glyphs, can’t take an active state, land on different baselines, and change shape per OS. Nothing in the UI can be tinted, sized or aligned reliably.
06
Secondary text fails contrastFONTOS
--color-muted #5a7a96 on --color-bg #07101e is 4.23:1 — below the 4.5:1 floor. It carries locations, times, nav labels and every caption, mostly at 10–11px. Grandparents are a stated user group.
07
Everything is the same cardFONTOS
.leg-card is reused for legs, other people’s legs, orphans, events and schedule templates. A ride that needs a decision and a ride that is settled differ only by a red hairline. Priority is expressed nowhere in size, position or weight.
08
Setup lives in the primary nav; settings live nowhereFONTOS
Sablon — a once-a-term configuration screen, admin-only — holds a fifth of the tab bar. Beállítások, a full screen with locations, routes, groups, breaks and push, is reachable only from a 36px gear repeated in every header.
09
The Hét grid is a spreadsheet on a phoneKISEBB
A 7-person × 7-day table with min-width:480 and 9–10px type, scrolled horizontally on a 360px screen, with drag-and-drop as the only way to move an event. Drag needs a mouse; the target users are on phones.
10
Two floating buttons over the contentKISEBB
“🤒 Beteg” and “⚡” stack above the nav on Ma, covering the last orphan card. ⚡ means “gyors rögzítés”, which is itself a modal offering two unrelated actions (cancel programmes / add a note).

The gap is the headline
One open question at a time, stated as a question, with the whole household’s faces underneath. One tap assigns. “1 / 2” tells you another is behind it. The card is the tallest, brightest thing on the screen because it is the only thing that needs you.
Conflicts are on the face, not in a panel
Papi already has a ride at 17:20, so Papi is dimmed and badged here rather than in a sub-step you only reach after choosing him. hasConflict and hasGoogleConflict already exist in Fuvartabla.tsx — this only moves where they surface.
Time gutter instead of a spine
Departure times run down one column with the journey length under each, so the day reads as a sequence and the tight gap between two rides is an object, not a decoration between cards.
Plain Hungarian
„Te vezetsz”, „Judit viszi, te is mész”, „Papi és Mami 2 fuvart visz ma”. No „láb”, no „gazdátlan”, no counts without a subject.
Four tabs, 72px, real icons
Hét absorbs Fuvartábla’s week view; Fuvarok becomes the queue of open assignments and carries the count as a badge; Események and Sablon and Beállítások collapse into Egyéb. Phosphor at 24px, labels at 12px, full-height targets.

Six signals in one 13px row
Time, travel estimate, a 📍? warning, a coloured arrow chip, the title, the child’s name in the child’s colour, and ⋯ — then a second line of locations and a third for the transfer hint. Everything is 10–13px and nothing is subordinate to anything else.
The picker pushes the week down
Opening it inserts ~260px inline, so the rows you were comparing against leave the screen at the moment you need them. A sheet would keep the list stable; better still, no panel at all.
Two full-width options before any person
“Önállóan” and “Gazdátlan hagyás” take the first two rows of the grid, ahead of the five drivers — the rare cases lead, and the destructive one (“leave it unassigned”) is styled as a neutral choice.
Three overlapping grouping concepts
„Körút” (merged trip), „Átszállítás” (transfer) and „Visszahozza is” (paired return leg) all mean “one driver does several of these”, and are expressed as a link icon, a lightning bolt and a checkbox in step two. One idea, three mechanics, three vocabularies.
Merge mode is a hidden mode
🔗 in the header silently turns every card into a checkbox row and swaps the bottom bar. There is no title change and no undo — and its only affordance is an emoji shared with the „Körút” label.

Unassigned cards carry their own picker
No accordion, no step two. The five faces are always visible on a card that needs a driver; assigned cards show who, with a quiet “Csere”. Nothing moves when you decide.
Companions become a second tap, not a second screen
Tap one face to assign, tap a second to add a companion — the same gesture, the same row. The current “max 2 · Judit vezet” counter and back-arrow disappear.
One word for one idea
Körút, átszállítás and the paired-return checkbox collapse into „Egy fuvarban” — a card that lists the stops in order. The merge affordance becomes a suggestion on the card that could join („17:35-kor Simit is lehet hozni”) rather than a hidden selection mode.
The week strip carries load, not just dates
A bar under each day: red where something has no driver, muted where it is covered. It replaces both the ◀ Ma ▶ paging and the „3 gazdátlan láb ezen a héten” banner, and tells you which day to open.
Direction as words
„Odaút” / „Hazaút” instead of a green → and a blue ← chip whose colours are the only difference and whose meaning has to be learnt.

A whole tab for “one-off things”, which the user experiences as the same thing as everything else on Hét — and where the entries carry no driver at all, so a new event silently becomes tomorrow’s orphan without saying so. The distinction that earns it a tab is template_id is null.
The IA these three imply
Hét, Fuvartábla and Események are three views of one list, split by data type rather than by task. Fold them into Hét (what is happening, with drivers shown inline), Fuvarok (only the rides that still need a decision), and a single + that adds either a one-off or a recurring item — the recurrence toggle is one field, not a separate tab. Sablon and Beállítások move together under a fourth “Egyéb”.

Where I would start

1 · EGY NAP
Make the orphan cards on Ma open the driver picker. Raise --color-muted. Retire „láb”. Three edits, and the app stops being a dead end.
2 · EGY HÉT
Replace the two-step picker with the always-visible face row (1b), with conflicts on the avatar. Keep the existing conflict functions; only the surface changes.
3 · EGY SPRINT
Collapse five tabs to four: Hét absorbs Fuvartábla’s week and Események; Fuvarok becomes the open-decision queue; Sablon and Beállítások move under Egyéb. Swap emoji for Phosphor in the same pass.
4 · KÉSŐBB
Unify körút / átszállítás / paired return into one “Egy fuvarban” object, and drop the horizon generator button by running it on template save.
Open questions for you: is the grandparent role expected to assign drivers, or only to be assigned? Is „Sablon” ever touched outside the start of a school term? And does the household actually use trips (körút), or was it built ahead of the need? Each one changes what the four tabs should be.
